ABOUT

I care about how things actually work.

I have spent my career working inside systems where complexity matters: military communications, cyber operations, enterprise security, Zero Trust architecture, software, and organizations trying to turn ambitious ideas into operational capability.

I tend to approach problems from first principles. What is the system trying to accomplish? What conditions must be true? Where does trust exist? Where does control exist? What is creating friction? What can be simplified without losing what matters?

Asymmetric Precision is the phrase I use for that style of thinking: finding the smallest, clearest, highest-leverage actions that improve the whole system.
